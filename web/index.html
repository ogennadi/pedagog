    Pedagog is a single-file pedagogical chess engine. Levels are calibrated against the Maia bots and read as Lichess classical ratings: level 2 ≈ 945, level 10 ≈ 1755 (Maia-1900); level 1 plays random moves. In standard chess the bots open from a small weighted book, at most six plies deep and wide on purpose (about a seventh of each side's first moves are rim or Grob-style flank thrusts), then the engine takes over. Each game draws its own engine seed, recorded in the PGN as SkillSeed. Click a move, or use the ← → keys, to step through the game. Board by chessground (GPL-3), rules by cm-chess (MIT). Works offline once loaded. Third-party license notices.
